Script

Every December, my grandmother sets up a small wooden stall at the edge of the farmers market, selling ornaments she carves herself from fallen pine branches behind her house. She says she gets an itch to start carving the moment the first cold morning arrives, long before anyone else is thinking about the holidays. This year, my cousin convinced her to track orders electronically instead of writing them by hand in a notebook, mostly so we could finally tell which ornaments actually sold out first. My grandmother agreed, grumbling the whole time about computers, though I noticed she checked the little tablet screen more often than she checked her own watch. By the final evening of the market, nearly every pine ornament was gone, and we sat together behind the empty stall, letting our tired eyes gaze out over the string lights strung across the square. She admitted, quietly, that the electronic order list had actually made things easier, though she made me promise never to tell anyone she had said so.

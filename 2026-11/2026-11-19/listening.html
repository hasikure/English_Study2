Script

My nephew has been interested in rockets and planets since he was about six years old, so last weekend I finally took him to the small Air and Space Museum two towns over. The highlight for him was an enormous model of a meteor crater, complete with a recording that explained exactly how much force it takes to carve a hole that size into solid rock. He must have listened to that recording four times before I could pull him away. Near the exit, there was also an old decommissioned missile standing upright behind a rope, something the museum had received decades ago from a nearby military base that eventually closed down. What surprised me more than any of the exhibits, honestly, was a small table by the gift shop collecting donations for a local campaign to keep the museum open; apparently rising rent nearly forced it to close last year. My nephew, without me suggesting anything, emptied his entire allowance into the donation box, saying it was for a good cause. I'm not sure he fully understood what he was supporting, but I have never seen him care about anything so completely in his life.

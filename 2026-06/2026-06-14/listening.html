Script

This weekend my niece turned seven, so my sister asked me to help with the party. The theme was dinosaurs, and we decided to make most of the decorations ourselves instead of buying them. I spent Friday evening cutting shapes out of cardboard and painting them green, which was messier than I expected.

The funny part was the cake. My niece insisted that her toy dinosaur be the model, and she had even named it after a real species she had read about. So I tried to copy its long neck and tiny head as closely as I could. It was not perfect, but for a homemade cake it would do.

On the day, about ten children arrived, most of them dressed up as different animals. We were a little worried, because the weather forecast had been bad all week, but in the end we managed to get by with games indoors. The children did not seem to mind at all.

Afterwards, my sister asked whether I had found the whole thing exhausting. Honestly, parts of it were. But watching my niece explain to her friends which facts about dinosaurs were actually relevant to her cake — that made the messy kitchen completely worth it.

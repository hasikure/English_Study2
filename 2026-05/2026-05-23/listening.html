The presenter was standing outside a nightclub district, where the city had opened a small late-night healthcare booth for young people. At first, I thought the idea sounded a little strange. Then a nurse explained that weekend injuries and panic attacks rise almost in proportion to the number of visitors in that area after midnight.

She said the booth was not there to punish anyone. In short, it was meant to give quick help before a small problem became a trip to the emergency room. People could ask for water, talk to a nurse, or rest for a few minutes with their friends. The report ended with a student saying she felt safer knowing that someone was nearby if things suddenly went wrong.

The whole segment lasted less than five minutes, but it changed my mind. I had never connected nightlife with public healthcare so directly before.

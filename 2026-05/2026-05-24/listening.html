Yesterday afternoon, I helped at a community donation event inside a small bookstore. Near the entrance, a volunteer had set up a rack where people could try on jackets and scarves before choosing what to take home. In the back, the owner's daughter-in-law was organizing used novels beside a table of school supplies.

One customer became excited when she found a signed bestseller under a pile of magazines. She held it up, and everyone around her laughed. The owner smiled too, but he watched the book almost jealously for a moment before deciding to donate it to the charity shelf instead of selling it online. He said he wanted the event to feel generous, not competitive.

By closing time, the room was almost empty. The signed book was gone, three children had left in warm coats, and the owner told me the event had gone better than expected.

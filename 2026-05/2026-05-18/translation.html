Ideal Version

This morning, the interview I had booked online was suddenly changed to an in-person one, so I wrote down the points I wanted to mention once more before leaving home. My train to the station was a little late, but I used that time to review the main points calmly, and by the time I arrived at the venue, I felt more confident than before. Even when something changes unexpectedly, the result can improve if you adjust the way you prepare.

Focus

  • was changed to ... is a useful passive pattern for schedules or formats that are changed by someone else.
  • by the time ... helps show that your feeling or situation had already changed when you arrived.
  • adjust the way you prepare is a practical way to express 「準備の仕方を少し変える」.

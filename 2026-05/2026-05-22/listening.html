This morning, I visited a neighborhood repair lab with my younger cousin. He wants to join the summer program there, so we went to ask a few questions before lunch. The room was quiet, and every workbench was covered with labeled tools, torn bags, and old desk lamps waiting to be fixed.

Ms. Sato, the instructor, told us that students who attend eight sessions will be eligible for a free starter kit. My cousin gave a small sigh when he heard that, because he had hoped he could get it after only two classes. Ms. Sato laughed and turned a damaged backpack inside out to show us how the lessons worked. She said the goal was not just to repair objects, but to help students cope with mistakes calmly and try again.

By the end of the visit, my cousin looked much more excited. On the way home, he said the place felt serious but kind, which sounded exactly right to me.

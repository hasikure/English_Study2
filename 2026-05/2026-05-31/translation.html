Ideal Version

  1. He would rather ask his colleagues for help than try to solve the problem on his own.
  2. It is not until people go abroad that many of them realise how unique their own culture is.

Focus

  • would rather A than B: 「BよりAしたい・BするよりAする方がましだ」。A・Bには動詞の原形が入る。否定は would rather not do、あるいは対比で would rather A than B とする。
  • It is not until ~ that ...: 「〜して初めて...する」。not until を強調構文（It-cleft）に入れた形。until の後には時を表す語句・節が来る。強調なしで書くと People do not realise ... until they go abroad となる。

Ideal Version

  1. As long as you keep practising a little every day, you are sure to improve.
  2. It takes years of experience and strong patience to become an excellent leader.

Focus

  • as long as: 「〜する限り」。条件を表す接続詞。"provided that" と同義に使えるが、より口語的。主節が現在形・未来形でも as long as 節は現在形を使う点に注意（例: as long as you try, not "as long as you will try"）。
  • It takes [time/effort] to do: 「〜するには〔時間・労力〕がかかる・必要だ」。時間・労力・資質などを主語に取れる汎用構文。"It takes courage to speak up." のように資質を表す名詞も使える。

Ideal Version

  1. It is not until you try it yourself that you realise how difficult it is.
  2. He did not even contact us in advance, let alone attend the meeting.

Focus

  • It is not until A that B: 「Aして初めてBする」。強調構文で時間的・条件的な順序を際立たせる。until の後には節（S+V）または名詞句が続く（例: It is not until you fail that you learn）。
  • let alone: 「〜はおろか」。否定文の後に置いて「より困難・大きなことはなおさら（無理だ）」を表す。A not/never ... let alone B の語順。

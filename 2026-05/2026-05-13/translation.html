Ideal Version

This morning, someone from the management company was supposed to come and check my broken air conditioner, so I tidied up the room before leaving. I had heard that the repair might take until the afternoon, but it was finished in about an hour because they only had to replace one part. I think I'll write down the model number and the contact information in case the same kind of problem comes up again.

Focus

  • be supposed to + verb is useful for 〜することになっていた: was supposed to come.
  • only had to + verb is a natural way to express 〜するだけで済んだ.
  • in case ... works well for 〜のために / 〜に備えて when you prepare for a future possibility.
